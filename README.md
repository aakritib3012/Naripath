# NariPath

**A community-led digital inclusion initiative for young women in Nepal.**

## About

NariPath is a proposed initiative designed to help young women discover educational and career opportunities, explore digital career pathways, develop practical skills, and access relevant guidance.

## Project Goals

- Make relevant opportunities easier to discover.
- Explain technology and digital careers, including non-coding pathways.
- Provide practical application resources.
- Explore mentorship and community-based support.
- Improve digital opportunity access through research-informed resources.

## Initial Website Features

- Homepage
- Opportunity directory
- Digital career pathways
- Application toolkit
- About and contact information

## Current Status

Early development. Research, website implementation, and outreach are in progress.

## Technology

- HTML
- CSS

## Project Principles

- Verify opportunity information before publishing.
- Protect participant privacy.
- Represent research findings honestly.
- Do not claim partnerships or results without evidence.

Code, changes, and development tasks are tracked through GitHub.

## Status of Claims

NariPath is a proposed initiative under development. The goals above describe intended work, not completed outcomes.
